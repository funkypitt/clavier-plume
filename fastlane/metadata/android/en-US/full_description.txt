Clavier Plume is a French/English Android keyboard derived from FUTO Keyboard, focused on writing French well. Offline, no ads, no telemetry: nothing leaves the device.

- Correct elision: l', d', qu', j', n', s'… generated by grammar and checked against the Dicollecte dictionary, not guessed ("l'hôtel" yes, "l'haricot" no).
- Accents restored: "voila" → "voilà", "coeur" → "cœur", "aplus" → "à plus".
- Imperatives and inversions: "rappelle-moi", "dépêche-toi", "allons-y", "a-t-il", "avez-vous".
- Typography: curly apostrophe, non-breaking spaces before ; : ! ?, « » quotation marks.
- No capital after abbreviations ("etc.", "cf.", "p. ex.", "M.").
- Two languages, one key: the EN/FR key switches instantly, so does swiping the space bar; both dictionaries stay active.
- Cautious learning: an unknown word is kept only on its second validation, a word deleted with backspace is unlearned, the "Learned words" screen lets you forget with one gesture.
- Dictionary built from Lexique 3.83, Leipzig corpus proper nouns and bigrams, LanguageTool vocabulary, and the passive vocabulary of the Littré.
- Minimal settings, offline dictation (Whisper), swipe typing, clipboard, nine themes including two high-contrast "ink" themes.
